Silence lock-screen test

A temporary page to see whether an iPhone keeps playing Silence's sound with the screen locked, when it plays as an ordinary looping audio file.

  1. Turn your phone's volume low, then press Start.
  2. Lock the phone and listen for about ten minutes.
  3. Unlock and screenshot this page. The log shows what happened while it was locked.
  4. Optional second run: tick the box below, start again and lock. The sound should stop after two minutes. That tests whether a sleep timer works while locked.

Volume here follows the phone's buttons; the page can't set it.
