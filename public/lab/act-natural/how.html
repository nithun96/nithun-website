How to play Act Natural

The TV shows a room full of identical blobs. Most of them are bots, wandering about at random. A few are you and your friends, steering with your phones. One player is the Hunter, who shoots at the blobs they think are human.

Two of these blobs are people. Which two?

1Join

  1. Scan the QR code on the TV with your phone camera, or open the link and type the five-letter room code.
  2. Type your name. Your phone is now your controller.
  3. Keep your screen to yourself. Its map shows where your blob is.

2Each round

A round lasts 90 seconds. One player is the Hunter. Everyone else is hiding as a blob in the crowd. After each round the next player in join order becomes the Hunter, so a full game is one round per player.

If you're hiding

  • Drag the joystick on your phone to walk. Every blob walks at the same speed, so you can't hurry.
  • Your phone shows three landmarks to visit in order, such as Fountain, then Bench, then Rug.
  • The map at the top of your phone shows where you are and rings your next landmark.
  • Stay inside the ring for 1.5 seconds to tick it off. Step out and the count starts again.

If you're the Hunter

  • You don't have a blob. Watch the TV like everyone else and look for the blob that isn't quite a bot.
  • Your phone shows every blob. Tap one to aim: a crosshair appears on it on the TV, so you can check it's the right one.
  • Press Shoot. You start with one shot and get another every 20 seconds, up to three at a time. Hit a bot and you can't shoot for 5 seconds.
  • Press Mark to tag up to three suspects. Everyone sees the numbered tags on the TV, so the room can follow your hunt. Mark again to remove one.
  • Hit a human and they're out for the rest of the round. Hit a bot and the shot is wasted. That bot disappears.

3Act natural

The Hunter is watching for anything a bot wouldn't do. Bots:

  • walk to a landmark about four times in ten, and to a random spot the rest of the time
  • stop for half a second to three seconds when they arrive, then pick somewhere new
  • drift a little as they walk, and sometimes pause halfway

So don't march straight from task to task, and don't stand still for long. Take a detour now and then. And don't stare at your phone when the Hunter looks at you.

4Scoring

The round ends when time runs out, when every human is out, or when everyone still in has finished their landmarks. The TV then shows who was who, and the scores. Scores stay off the TV during a round, so a score going up can't give anyone away.

Between rounds the host can change the map with M: the park, Vietnam, Jaffna, East Oslo or Ålesund. The landmarks change; the rules don't.

The host starts each round with Enter on the computer that runs the TV. Everyone is the Hunter once; after the last round come the final scores. Between rounds the host can also remove a player with the Kick button beside their name.
