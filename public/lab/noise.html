What to test (iPhone, Safari or Firefox):

  1. Turn the ring/mute switch to silent, tap Play. Do you hear it?
  2. Lock the phone for a minute. Does the sound keep going? Is there a pause button on the lock screen?
  3. Unlock and come back here: "Kept up while away" should say yes.
  4. Switch to another app for a minute, then come back. Same check.
  5. Try the volume slider and the fade-out.
